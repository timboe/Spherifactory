[H+] flows outward from the Sun. A building that pulls resources outward can 
capture it for you.

Extractors draw resources from the ring below and push them outward. Build one 
on the one available ring to feed [H+] to a waiting ship.

Build a building on the ring. Click on it and make it an Extractor. Choose [H+] 
as the extracted resource.

[Hi+] is made from [H+] and [St-]. [Ir-] arrives on the outer rings and flows 
inward, look for the building type that pulls [-] resources in that direction.

Build a [St-] factory ([Ir-]→[St-]) on the 
ring 1 below or 2 below the [Ir-]. Then build a [Hi+] factory ([H+]+[St-]→[Hi+]) 
where outward-flowing [H+] and inward-flowing [St-] are also both 1 or 2 rings
above the factory.

Inject [Ir-] on Ring 5 (outmost), then Build a [St-] factory on Ring 4 to deposit [St-] 
on Ring 3. Build a [Hi+] factory on Ring 1 and supply it additionally with [H+]
by building a [H+] extractor on Ring 1. Finish by constructing [Hi+] extractors
on Rings 3 and 5.
